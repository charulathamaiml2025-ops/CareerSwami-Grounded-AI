CAREERSWAMI STUDENT CLUB - OFFICIAL HANDBOOK
Version: 1.0
Source of Truth Policy: This handbook is the sole authoritative source for club policies and logistics.

SECTION 1: SCHEDULE AND FORMAT
1. Club sessions happen every Wednesday.
2. Session time is 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
3. Sessions are conducted remotely.

SECTION 2: REGISTRATION AND CAPACITY
4. Registration closes every Tuesday at 6:00 PM.
5. Maximum capacity is 20 participants.
6. Beginners are welcome.

SECTION 3: WAITLIST AND CANCELLATIONS
7. If the club is full, users can join a waitlist.
8. Joining the waitlist does NOT guarantee a place.
9. Cancellations must be requested 24 hours ahead.

SECTION 4: FEES AND ASSISTANCE
10. There are no fees.
11. Users can contact the club coordinator for assistance.
